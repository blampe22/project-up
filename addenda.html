The Project Architect wishes to clarify that his initial quality assessment may have been somewhat premature.

Specifically, the Architect's first review consisted primarily of confirming that the website existed.

Subsequent field observations revealed that the website was, in fact, quite good.

Nevertheless, several minor deficiencies were identified and have been documented for correction prior to Owner review.

The Owner has not yet accepted this interpretation.
