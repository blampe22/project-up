THANKSGIVING PROJECT PREVIEW

Meet the Project Architect!

Curious about Project UP? Join us during the Thanksgiving holidays for an informal look at the mural, painting opportunities, and how you can become part of Auggie and Theo's adventure.

Proposed · Christmas 2026

Junior Painters Christmas Crafts Event

Painting materials are anticipated before Christmas 2026. If materials arrive and family schedules allow, the Project Team hopes to host an informal Christmas crafts event. A date and time are still open.

Junior Painters, parents, and other family members are welcome. Adults will supervise younger painters using suitable materials on designated panels or age-appropriate practice pieces. Participation is voluntary and all experience levels are welcome.

Creativity, different painting techniques, and the occasional crooked brushstroke are welcome in a family-built masterpiece.

About the Project

A Note from the Project Architect

For those wondering why a nursery painting project has a Project Manual, subcontractor registration, procurement logs, a proposed budget, and a construction schedule...

The Project Architect happens to be a General Contractor by trade.

And the Owner? She's an Actuary.

If you've ever wondered what happens when a General Contractor marries an Actuary, apparently you get a nursery mural that's been planned, quantified, risk-assessed, modeled, validated, and thoroughly documented.

Every assumption has been questioned. Every contingency has been considered. And every good idea has somehow become a spreadsheet.

The Project Architect is responsible for figuring out how to build it.

The Owner is responsible for making sure the plan actually holds up.

A Preview of the Owner's Budget Review

Owner: "What's this contingency allowance?"

Project Architect: "Unforeseen conditions."

Owner: "You designed the entire project."

Project Architect: "Exactly. You should see the unforeseen conditions."

Owner: "And what's this line item for miscellaneous materials?"

Project Architect: "I'd prefer to discuss that in executive session."

Owner: "Your fee is being reduced."

Project Architect: "I'm working for free!"

Owner: "Then we're making excellent progress on value engineering."

Please note: The above exchange is a hypothetical projection. Actual Owner comments may be considerably more thorough.

Welcome to Project UP — where imagination meets probability, and even the fun has a contingency allowance. 🎈
