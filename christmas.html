Inspired by the heart of Up, we're reminded that life's greatest adventures aren't always about the places we go, but the people who share the journey with us.

It feels like just yesterday that you all came together to celebrate our wedding. And now, less than a year after that wonderful celebration, we're preparing to welcome two little adventurers into our family!

Following our Block Letter project for Auggie and Theo, we've received a few requests from some of our aspiring Junior Painters to help with more nursery projects.

Well, the Project Architect has been hard at work!

Adventure Begins at Home

Inspired by our love, appreciation, and devotion to family, we're excited to introduce Project UP — Adventure Begins at Home!

Using a little imagination, our 3D printer, and some help from artificial intelligence, we're creating a one-of-a-kind nursery mural made up of 40 individually printed paint-by-number panels.

And we'd love for our family to help bring it to life.

We're hoping to bring everyone together again this Christmas for a little painting and a lot of memories. Hopefully with a little less ice on the ground than our wedding—although a white Christmas could make for an adventure of its own!

We just need to make sure the Project Architect has better footing this time around.

40 Pieces. One Big Adventure.

We've taken a special piece of artwork and divided it into 40 individual panels. With a little 3D printing magic, each panel becomes a paint-by-number project that someone in our family can bring to life.

See How the Mural Comes Together

Painting together

A Christmas 2026 painting get-together is a possibility, depending on family availability, materials, and when the boys arrive. Participation is optional. Junior Painters are welcome with a parent or guardian.

During the Thanksgiving holidays, the Project Architect can show you the design, answer questions, and help you sign up if you’d like.

More than anything, we want this to be something Auggie and Theo can grow up with, knowing how many people loved them before they were even born.

My Adventure Photo Album

Volume 1: Before Our Adventure Began

If you’d like, you can contribute photos of your painting experience or a portrait with your completed panel to this private family keepsake. The album will help the boys discover who helped create their nursery. We’ll honor existing photography preferences and ask for parent or guardian permission for minors.

We're grateful for the love and support you've shown us throughout this incredible year.

We love you all, and we can't wait to begin this next chapter with every single one of you.

Because the greatest adventure isn't the one we take alone. It's the one we share with the people we love.
